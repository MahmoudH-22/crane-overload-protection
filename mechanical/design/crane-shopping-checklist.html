Milestone 1: what has to work

Both prototypes must work on the physical crane model, not on a bare breadboard:

ON/OFF sensor: the limit switch under the arm tip is pressed by the rising hook, and the app shows AT TOP / BELOW TOP every time.

Analog sensor (with conditioning): the 5 kg load cell hangs between the rope and the basket on the Z-mount. Its 0–5 mV signal goes through your INA125 amplifier (gain resistor, 0.42 V reference, RC filter) into the Uno, and the app shows the weight in kg. Check it against the kitchen scale with the test bottles.

Also needed: the laser-cut MDF frame (base, mast, arm, brace, gussets, motor plate, switch bracket). The electronics can stay on the breadboard for M1.

Not needed yet: current sensor (ACS712), LCD, control panel parts, PCB, buzzer, paint.

Check before you pay

Motor: it must come with an encoder, run on 12 V, at ≈ 20 RPM, with ≥ 8 kg·cm torque and a Ø6 mm D-shaft.

Load cell: read the mV/V rating on its label to choose the gain resistor (1 mV/V → 91 Ω, 2 mV/V → 180 Ω).

STOP button: it needs an NC contact (the 3-pin 1NO1NC type).

Buck converter: set it to 5.00 V with a multimeter before connecting anything.

No longer needed: IR module, ultrasonic sensor, separate encoder, flexible coupler, stub shaft, encoder bracket.
