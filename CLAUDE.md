# Crane Overload: MCT332s (Fall 2026, Ain Shams)

Tabletop crane that weighs its load, stops before overload (1.5 kg), stops at the top of travel, and detects a jammed motor. Operated from a control panel or remotely from the phone app (course requirement: lift / lower / stop from the app + live readings).

Team: Mahmoud Hisham 2400844, Ziad Hany 2301401, Abdulrahman Mohamed 2400544, Ahmed Hany 2400311.

## System
```
Sensors → Arduino Uno (ALL safety logic, 50 ms loop)
           ⇅ SoftwareSerial 9600 baud, XOR-checksum lines (crane_link.h)
         HC-05 Bluetooth module (transparent serial, no decisions)
           ⇅ Bluetooth Classic SPP, ~10 m
         Flutter app (app/, Android): parses the lines itself (crane_link_codec.dart)

Development without the crane: Flutter app (Wi-Fi) ⇄ Mosquitto on the laptop ⇄ tools/crane_sim.py
```
- Since 21-11-2026 the HC-05 replaces the NodeMCU Wi-Fi/MQTT bridge (same Uno pins, same lines). The app does the
  NodeMCU's old job (line ⇄ topic + JSON) inside `BluetoothGateway`, so everything above the connection layer still
  speaks the MQTT contract below. HC-05 = Bluetooth Classic: **Android only** (iOS has no SPP).
- Sensors: load cell + AD620 (kg), ACS712 (motor A), IR module (top of travel), motor Hall encoder (hook height, mm), ultrasonic HC-SR04 under the basket pointing down (obstacle below the load).
- **The app never makes safety decisions.** The Uno enforces overload, top stop, jam and link loss; the app only shows state and sends requests the Uno may refuse.

## MQTT contract (source of truth: app, simulator and firmware must match)
The app's internal message contract on both links. Over Bluetooth the topics / JSON are produced by the app from the Uno lines (QoS, retain and Last Will do not apply; `crane/status` is generated by `BluetoothGateway`: online while valid lines arrive, offline after 2 s of silence, `fw` = `uno-bt`). With the simulator it is real MQTT:
Broker `tcp://<laptop-ip>:1883`, no TLS, no auth. UTF-8 JSON. QoS 0 live data, QoS 1 commands / events.

| Topic | Dir | QoS | Payload |
|---|---|---|---|
| `crane/live` | crane→app, ~5 Hz | 0 | `{"t":123456,"kg":1.234,"max":1.5,"h":212.5,"a":0.42,"top":false,"st":"LIFTING","mode":"REMOTE","src":"APP","us":180.0,"obs":false}` |
| `crane/cmd` | app→crane | 1 | `{"id":17,"cmd":"LIFT"}`; cmd ∈ LIFT, LOWER, GOTO (+`"value":200` mm), STOP, RESET, TARE, SET_MAX (+`"value":1.2`), PING |
| `crane/ack` | crane→app | 1 | `{"id":17,"ok":false,"reason":"OVERLOAD"}`; reasons OVERLOAD, JAM, TOP, ESTOP, LOCAL_MODE, BUSY, RANGE, OBSTACLE, NOT_FITTED |
| `crane/events` | crane→app | 1 | `{"t":123999,"type":"OVERLOAD","kg":1.53}`; types OVERLOAD, OVERLOAD_CLEARED, JAM, TOP, ESTOP, ESTOP_RELEASED, RESET, TARE, MAX_CHANGED, LINK_LOST, OBSTACLE, OBSTACLE_CLEARED |
| `crane/status` | crane→app, retained | 1 | `{"online":true,"fw":"m2.0","sensors":["kg","top"]}`; Last Will `{"online":false}` retained |

- `st`: IDLE | LIFTING | LOWERING | OVERLOAD | JAM | TOP | ESTOP. `mode`: LOCAL | REMOTE. `src`: APP | PANEL | NONE. `h`: 0 = table, ≈ 346 = top.
- `id` increments per message, int, wraps 65535 → 0.
- **Hold-to-run:** app repeats LIFT/LOWER every 250 ms while held; crane stops if no repeat within 600 ms, and stops app-started motion if no message at all for 2 s. App sends STOP on release, cancel, slide-off, app background, disconnect.
- **GOTO (go to height)** uses the same keep-alive as hold-to-run: the app repeats `{"cmd":"GOTO","value":<mm>}` every 250 ms for as long as the move should run (the user taps GO once; the app sends the repeats by itself until arrival, CANCEL/STOP, a blocked condition, app background or disconnect). The crane drives toward the target (slowing near it), stops by itself within 3 mm using the encoder, and stays stopped while repeats keep coming. Same 600 ms / 2 s timeouts; STOP on release. Target 5…346 mm, otherwise ack RANGE. Upward GOTO is refused like LIFT (OVERLOAD, TOP, JAM, ESTOP, LOCAL_MODE); downward like LOWER. A new value while moving retargets. No extra ack or event on arrival.
- **Obstacle under the load (ultrasonic):** `us` = distance below the basket in mm (null if no echo or closer than ~20 mm). The Uno compares it with the expected clearance from the encoder height: obstacle if `us` < `h` − 30 mm (or no echo) while `h` ≥ 50 mm. Set after 2 consecutive readings, cleared by itself after 0.5 s clear (no RESET). While `obs` is true: LOWER and downward GOTO are refused (`OBSTACLE`), and a lowering in progress stops at once (event OBSTACLE). LIFT stays allowed (except at the top, as always). `st` is unchanged (e.g. IDLE).
- **Partly built crane:** `h`, `a`, `us` are `null` when that sensor is not fitted. `crane/status.sensors` lists what is fitted: `kg`, `top`, `h`, `a`, `us`, `motor` (absent = not reported, assume all, e.g. the simulator). Without `motor`, LIFT / LOWER / GOTO are refused with `NOT_FITTED`. `online:false` when the Uno has been silent for 2 s (set by the app's `BluetoothGateway`; formerly the NodeMCU).
- (Old NodeMCU) PubSubClient publishes at QoS 0 only; the app subscribes at QoS 1 and works either way.
- PING every 1 s while connected.
- RESET accepted only below 95 % of max. TARE only when IDLE. SET_MAX 0.5…1.5 kg (crane clamps to 1.5; below 0.5 → RANGE).
- `ack` only for LIFT/LOWER refusals and RESET / TARE / SET_MAX results (simulator acks a repeated refusal at most once per second).
- MAX_CHANGED event: `kg` carries the new limit.
- (Old NodeMCU) Use MQTT keepalive ≈ 2 s on the crane side so the broker publishes the Last Will within ~3 s of a power cut.

## Uno serial link (`crane_link.h`), carried by the HC-05
SoftwareSerial 9600, Uno D10 (RX) ← HC-05 TXD, Uno D11 (TX) → 1 k → HC-05 RXD with 2 k to GND (3.3 V input), HC-05 VCC 5 V, common GND. Pair the HC-05 in Android settings (PIN 1234 / 0000). (The old NodeMCU used the same pins: D5 RX via the divider, D6 TX.)
Each line `<body>*XX\n` (XOR checksum, hex). Empty field = no value -> JSON null.
- Uno -> app: `L,t,kg,max,h,a,top,st,mode,src,us,obs` (200 ms) -> crane/live; `A,id,ok,reason` -> crane/ack; `E,t,type,kg` -> crane/events; `S,kg top ...` (2 s) -> crane/status sensors.
- App -> Uno: `C,id,CMD[,value]` (known commands only, id 0…65535, value with 3 decimals; PING included).
- Lines with a bad checksum or invalid fields are dropped (`CraneLinkCodec`, same checks as the C++ header). The app only translates; the Uno decides (overload latch, RESET < 95 %, TARE only IDLE, SET_MAX 0.5-1.5, NOT_FITTED).

- Rules (all pure, unit-tested):
  - Link health: Live if `crane/live` ≤ 1 s old; Stale 1–3 s; Crane offline if `status.online=false` or > 3 s; Not connected if the link (Bluetooth or broker) is down.
  - Controls disabled when not connected, stale, offline, LOCAL, or ESTOP (STOP stays enabled whenever the link is up). LIFT disabled in OVERLOAD, JAM, TOP (or top sensor). LOWER disabled only in JAM. RESET only in OVERLOAD/JAM. TARE only IDLE.
  - If a held direction becomes disabled (overload, stale…), the app releases the hold and sends STOP.
  - LIFT (and upward GO) is also disabled when `kg` > `max` ("Load above max"), even before the crane latches OVERLOAD.
  - GO (go to height) is **tap-once**: tap GO → the app keeps sending GOTO by itself; the button turns into CANCEL. Disabled with no target ("Pick a height") or within 3 mm ("At target"); otherwise follows the LIFT rule for targets above the hook and the LOWER rule below (so: no object under the load when going down, load not above max / no OVERLOAD / not at top when going up, no JAM, ESTOP, LOCAL, stale data). If any of these becomes true during the move, the app stops it (STOP). On arrival: STOP, vibration, "Reached N mm", and the card resets (target cleared). CANCEL or a stop for another reason keeps the target. Moving the slider during the move retargets.
  - IR top sensor (`live.top`) shown under the height bar: amber "IR TOP" when active, grey "IR clear".
  - Obstacle (`live.obs`): LOWER and downward GO disabled ("Object under the load"); a held LOWER / downward GO is released with STOP; red banner + vibration (after ESTOP / JAM / OVERLOAD in priority). "Under the load" card shows the ultrasonic distance and CLEAR / OBJECT BELOW.
  - Gauge: green < 90 % of max, amber 90–100 %, red over max or OVERLOAD. Scale 0–2 kg.
  - Alerts (red banner + vibration): OVERLOAD, JAM, ESTOP, crane offline.
  - Not-fitted sensors (`fittedSensorsProvider` from `crane/status.sensors`): height bar "n/a" (no marker), IR "n/a", current / ultrasonic cards "not fitted"; no `motor` -> LIFT / LOWER / GO disabled "Motor not connected"; no height -> GO disabled "No height sensor". `CraneLive.heightMm` / `amps` are nullable.

## Mechanical key values (from the planning chats, 14-24 Nov 2026)
- Structure: 7 mm MDF, laser-cut finger-jointed box beams (68 pieces) + PETG 3D-printed parts. 740 × 300 × 618 mm overall, outreach ≈ 409 mm, hook travel 0-346 mm.
- Rated load 1.5 kg (proof 1.9 kg).
- Hoist: 25GA370 12 V 45:1 (see Open items), Ø10 printed drum on the Ø4 D-shaft, Ø7 journal in the outboard support (Ø7.4 hole, plain bearing), braided PE 0.6 mm rope, 5 dead turns.
- Pulley: printed Ø24 wheel (6.0 mm thick, flat faces) on a fixed Ø7 × 7.5 sleeve over a threaded M4 × 30, 2 spacer rings; U-bracket via 2 × M3 × 65 through the arm block stack.
- Load cell on two identical printed Z-parts (48 × 14 × 30), each end bolted to its own part only (M4 × 40 through-bolts), pins and bar centre on one vertical line.
- Checks: arm SF ≈ 37, deflection 0.46 mm, rope SF ≈ 14, Z-part SF ≈ 8, tipping SF ≈ 2.0 (estimated masses).
- Load cell conditioning: AD620AN RG 180 Ω (G ≈ 275); REF 1.56 V from 2.2 kΩ / 1 kΩ + LM358 follower; 16 kΩ + 1 µF film (≈ 10 Hz); 100 nF at AD620 pins 7-4.

## Conventions
- Clean code, clean architecture: small widgets, no business logic in widgets, repositories behind interfaces.
- A doc comment (`///` Dart, docstring Python, Doxygen C++) before every function and class: what it does, params, return.
- Line length 120 (`dart format -l 120 lib test`).
- Layout must never overflow: anything in a fixed-size box (hold / STOP / GO buttons, IR badge, top bar) uses `FittedBox(fit: BoxFit.scaleDown)`; text in rows uses `Expanded` + ellipsis. `test/dashboard_states_test.dart` renders every crane state at 320 / 360 / 393 px wide and with a 130 % font; add new states / widgets there.
- Uncaught async errors are logged (`PlatformDispatcher.onError` in `main.dart`); MQTT stream errors are handled in `MqttService`.
- Firmware: no `delay()` in loops; timing with `millis()`.
- Update this file and `history/YYYY-MM/DD-MM-YYYY.md` after any significant work.

## Key decisions
- The crane reports its fitted sensors (`crane/status.sensors`) so the same app works with the partly built crane, the full crane and the simulator; missing readings are null, never fake zeros.
- The serial protocol code lives in one plain C++ header (no Arduino dependency) so it is unit-tested on the laptop; the app's `CraneLinkCodec` (formerly the NodeMCU) validates every field (numbers / upper-case words only) before building JSON.
- Safety stays on the Uno; the app is a requester. Availability rules only avoid pointless requests.
- STOP fires on finger-down (not release) and is enabled whenever the link (Bluetooth or broker) is up, even when the crane looks offline.
- App background = any lifecycle state other than `resumed` (inactive, hidden, paused) → STOP. Safer than waiting for `paused`.
- No STOP is sent after a connection drop (impossible); the crane's 600 ms / 2 s timeouts cover it, and the hold does not resume after reconnect.
- Initial connect failures show an error; only a link that was up auto-reconnects with back-off.
- Freshness is measured from the connection time until the first sample, so "Crane offline" shows 3 s after connecting to a broker with no crane.
- `tools/` (simulator + its Mosquitto config) lives at the repo root; the Flutter project stays in `app/`. The old duplicate `broker/` folder was removed (22-11-2026).
- Go to height was first hold-to-run, then changed to tap-once at the team's request (16-11-2026). The finger dead-man is replaced by an app dead-man: the app sends the GOTO repeats itself, so if the phone freezes, loses Wi-Fi or the app goes to background, the repeats stop and the crane halts within 600 ms. The Uno still stops at the target (encoder at 50 ms is far more precise than 5 Hz telemetry) and still enforces every safety rule. No contract change was needed.

- Link to the phone: HC-05 Bluetooth instead of the NodeMCU (team decision 21-11-2026): no broker, laptop or hotspot needed for the demo; the Uno sketch did not change (same pins, same lines). The app keeps the MQTT contract internally and translates the lines itself, so telemetry / control / events and their tests did not change, and the MQTT path stays for the simulator. Cost: Android only, one phone at a time, ~10 m range.
- Milestone 1 prototypes: IR (ON/OFF) and load cell with the team's AD620 conditioning (HX711 not allowed by the course).
- AD620 REF kept at 2.2 kΩ (1.56 V) as built; 2.0 kΩ (1.67 V) would sit inside the AD620's 1.6 V REF minimum: recommended, not yet done.
- No ball bearings: printed pulley wheel on a fixed sleeve; drum journal in a printed support.
- 25GA370 spur gearmotor instead of the worm motor (availability).
