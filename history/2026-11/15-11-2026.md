## Decided
- Any non-resumed lifecycle state counts as background → STOP.
- STOP fires on finger-down and stays enabled whenever the broker is connected.
- Only a link that was up auto-reconnects; first-connect failures show an error.
- `tools/` at the repo root; Flutter project stays in `app/`.
