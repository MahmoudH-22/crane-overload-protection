## Full pin map (finished crane)

| Uno pin | Used for | Dir. | Why this pin | Now (m2) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D0 / D1 | USB serial (upload, Serial Monitor) | — | wired to the USB chip: **keep free** | same |
| **D2** | motor encoder C1 / A | in | external interrupt INT0 | IR sensor (moves to A2) |
| **D3** | motor encoder C2 / B | in | external interrupt INT1 | free |
| **D4** | ultrasonic Trig | out | free digital pin | free |
| **D5** | L298N ENA | out | PWM (speed) | free |
| **D6** | buzzer | out | free digital pin | free |
| **D7** | L298N IN1 (through the top limit switch) | out | direction | free |
| **D8** | HC-05 TXD → AltSoftSerial RX | in | AltSoftSerial's fixed RX pin | free |
| **D9** | AltSoftSerial TX → divider → HC-05 RXD | out | AltSoftSerial's fixed TX pin | free |
| **D10** | L298N IN2 | out | direction (no PWM needed) | HC-05 TXD (SoftwareSerial) |
| **D11** | ultrasonic Echo | in | pin-change interrupt possible | HC-05 RXD (SoftwareSerial) |
| **D12** | STOP button (NC to GND) | in | own pin: fast, fail-safe | free |
| D13 | built-in LED (status) | out | on the board | IR mirror |
| **A0** | load cell (AD620 via RC filter) | analog | ADC | same |
| **A1** | ACS712 motor current | analog | ADC | free |
| **A2** | IR top sensor | in | analog pins work as digital | free |
| **A3** | E-stop sense (12 V divider) | in | ADC / digital | free |
| **A4 / A5** | I2C SDA / SCL: LCD + PCF8574 | bus | the Uno's I2C pins | free |

All 18 usable pins are taken; **D13 is the only spare** (plus PCF8574 P7). Any new part goes on the I2C bus.

## Power: two supplies, one ground

### 12 V branch: the motor only
A **12 V ≥ 2 A adapter** → **power switch** → **E-stop (NC)** → L298N +12V → motor. The E-stop cuts this branch in hardware. The GA25-370 is a spur gearbox, so check that it holds the load without power (`05`, section 7).

### 5 V branch: the Uno and every sensor / module
The Uno's 5 V pin feeds everything else through the breadboard / PCB rails:

| Part | Typical current at 5 V |
|---|---|
| Arduino Uno | 50 mA |
| HC-05 | 30–40 mA |
| IR module | 20 mA |
| Load cell bridge + AD620 + LM358 | 16 mA |
| Motor Hall encoder | ≈ 10 mA |
| HC-SR04 | 15 mA |
| ACS712 | 10 mA |
| LCD with backlight | 20–25 mA |
| PCF8574 + 3 LEDs (~9 mA each) | 30 mA |
| Buzzer (when beeping) | 30 mA |
| **Total** | **≈ 250 mA** |

**How to power the Uno:**
- **On the bench:** USB from the laptop (500 mA) is enough.
- **Standalone:** the Uno's own regulator gets hot at 12 V in: (12 − 5) V × 0.25 A ≈ 1.8 W. Use a small **buck converter set to 7.5 V into the barrel jack** (only (7.5 − 5) × 0.25 ≈ 0.6 W in the regulator). USB can stay plugged in; the Uno switches over by itself.
- Never connect the L298N's own 5 V output to the Uno's 5 V.

### One common ground
12 V supply −, L298N GND, Uno GND and every module's GND are connected. Every signal (IN1, encoder pulses, serial, analog voltages) is measured against ground, so a missing ground is the most common cause of "random" behaviour. Join the motor ground and the Uno ground at **one point** (the L298N GND terminal), so motor current doesn't flow through the sensor ground wires.

### Decoupling
- 100 nF at each chip's supply pins (AD620 7–4) and **across the motor terminals**.
- 10 µF across the Uno 5V–GND.
- Optional: a **2 A fuse** right after the 12 V adapter.

## Logic levels

| Connection | Driver | Receiver | Problem? | Solution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Uno D11 → HC-05 RXD | 5 V | 3.3 V input | **yes** | divider 1 k / 1 k (2.5 V) or 1 k / 2 k (3.3 V) |
| HC-05 TXD → Uno D10 | 3.3 V | 5 V input | no (3.3 > 3.0 V threshold) | direct |
| Motor encoder C1 / C2 → D2 / D3 | Hall sensor (5 V supply) | 5 V input | only if the board has no pull-ups | 4.7 kΩ pull-ups to 5 V |
| E-stop 12 V → A3 | 12 V | max 5 V | **yes** | divider 10 k / 4.7 k → 3.8 V |
| IR, HC-SR04, buttons, L298N inputs | 5 V | 5 V | no | direct |
| AD620 → A0, ACS712 → A1 | 1.2–3.8 V / 2–3 V analog | 0–5 V ADC | no | RC filter on A0 |

## Still needed before the full crane works

**Parts to buy or confirm**
1. **Motor: 25GA370, 12 V, 45 : 1** (as in `mechanical/design/crane-structure-3d.html`): ≈ 1.9 kg·cm rated against ≈ 1.15 kg·cm needed on the Ø10 drum (safety factor ≈ 1.65), stall ≈ 1.2 A, within the L298N's 2 A. Measure the lifting and stall current to set the jam threshold (`05`, section 2).
2. **Mechanical drive:** follow the 3D design page (motor held by its face in printed bracket 06, drum 07 on the D-shaft, journal in outboard support 08). `mechanical/README.md`, `bom.csv` and the STEP files still show the old JGY-370 / Ø30 drum design.
3. **Holding test:** hang 1.5 kg, switch the 12 V off, and check how fast it sinks (45:1 spur gearbox, not self-locking).
4. **12 V ≥ 2 A adapter**, **buck converter** (12 → 7.5 V), **2 A fuse**.
5. **E-stop with an NC contact block**, **STOP button with an NC contact**, LIFT / LOWER / RESET (NO), **2-position key switch**, rocker power switch.
6. **PCF8574 I/O module**, **16×2 LCD with I2C backpack**, **active buzzer module**, 3 LEDs (green / amber / red).
7. **ACS712-5A**, **HC-SR04**, ~1 m of thin **4-core flexible cable** for the basket.
8. Resistors: 2 × 4.7 kΩ (encoder, if needed), 10 kΩ (IN1 pull-down), 10 kΩ + 4.7 kΩ (E-stop sense), 3 × 330 Ω (LEDs), a 2 kΩ for the HC-05 divider (ideal 3.3 V); 100 nF ceramics (motor, chips), screw terminals.

**Decisions**
9. **IR sensor and limit switch**: both are on the crane. Suggested use: **IR = software top stop** (state TOP, event to the app, homing reference), **limit switch = hardware backup in IN1** (`05`, section 3).
10. **Shield PCB (BOM CR-28)**: the BOM says "KiCad design; Gerbers in hardware/", but there is no `hardware/` folder in the repo. Either design it from this pin map, or use a perfboard.

**Firmware (crane_uno_m2)**
11. **Move the HC-05 to AltSoftSerial on D8 / D9** when the motor is added (SoftwareSerial would lose encoder pulses; `05`, section 5), then IN2 → D10, Echo → D11. Move the IR from D2 to **A2**. All in `config.h`.
12. New parts, each as a `.h` / `.cpp` pair (`firmware/uno/crane_uno_m2/README.md` explains how): motor + hold-to-run / GOTO with the 600 ms / 2 s timeouts and brake when stopped, encoder (interrupt on A rising, direction from B, homing at the IR top), ACS712 jam, ultrasonic obstacle (echo by pin-change interrupt or median of 3), panel (LCD, PCF8574, STOP, MODE, buzzer), E-stop sense. Libraries: `AltSoftSerial`, `LiquidCrystal_I2C`, a PCF8574 library (or plain `Wire`).
13. **Height reference (homing):** at power-up, lift slowly until the IR triggers and set h = 346 mm. Then measure the real pulses per mm once (mark 100 mm on the mast) and put that number in `config.h`.
